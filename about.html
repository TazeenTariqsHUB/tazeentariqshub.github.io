HI!

I'm Tazeen Tariq, a third-year Cyber Security student at Air University – NIIT and a Junior SQA Engineer. By day I break web apps and APIs on purpose so nobody else can. By night I write about how secure AI-written code and AI assistants really are.

I learn by building. A shark-tracking system for the NASA Space Apps Challenge taught me object detection from zero. An OSINT tool called DarkRecon taught me how much a domain gives away. A one-person research paper taught me that the scanners developers trust miss most of the flaws in LLM-generated code, usually because the security control was never written at all. That paper is under review now, and that question is the direction I'm heading in.

I'm the person people hand the microphone to. Class Representative, Media Society president at two institutions, a declamation finalist more than once, and an O-Level maths tutor while I was still a student myself. Leading a room and explaining a hard thing clearly are skills I take as seriously as code.

As a kid I failed subjects, except computer studies, which just made sense. That taught me early that effort is a choice, so I give it where it counts. Fashion is the other place I give it. Precision, creativity, and refusing to settle look the same in both.

Curious by default, careful on purpose, and happiest when I'm making something work that wasn't supposed to.
