Why Not Both?

Hi, I'm Tazeen Tariq — a Cybersecurity student, a QA Engineer, and a fashion enthusiast.

I know these interests may seem a little different from one another, and many people ask me, "If fashion, then why tech?" My answer has always been simple: Why not?

My journey from being a challanged child to this stage has taken many turns, but two things have remained constant: my passion for technology, which I pursue with the utmost perfection, and my love for clothes and fashion, which I approach with the same level of dedication.

As a child, I struggled with and even failed in multiple subjects. Yet, without putting in much effort, computer studies were always the subject I excelled in. It was almost natural for me. From nursery school to university, my journey has been filled with challenges. Growing up as a middle-class student in a big school, two of the biggest challenges I faced were pursuing technology at the highest possible level while also keeping up with the latest trends in fashion.

But limitations? No more.

I have always been the kind of person who sees things as challenges. No, I don't consider myself special, I simply have a mindset that pushes me to do whatever I choose to do to the highest possible standard.

That mindset is also one of the reasons I chose cybersecurity. In cybersecurity, even the tiniest mistake can create a gap, and that gap can have consequences. To me, that is not something to fear; it is a challenge. A challenge to be more precise, more careful, and more determined not to leave any gap behind.

As I have grown, I have equipped myself with a lot of knowledge, skills, and experience. Sometimes, what I lacked was guidance, but my willingness to learn, figure things out, and give everything my best has always made up for it.

I believe in following the rules, doing the work properly, and getting the job done. And, honestly, I have seen the results.

My story may not follow the most conventional path. Technology and fashion may seem like two completely different worlds, but for me, they represent the same thing: perfection, creativity, precision, and the courage to challenge myself.

So, if someone asks me again, "If fashion, then why tech?"

I'll still say: Why not both?
