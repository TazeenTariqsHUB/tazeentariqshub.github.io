PAPER 01

"Securing the AI-Driven Software Lifecycle: A Comparative Study of Vulnerability Detection in AI-Generated Code"

Empirical study of LLM-generated code security. Built a benchmark of ten LLM-generated Python programs from standardized prompts, measured Bandit and Semgrep static analysis against a manual security audit, classified the confirmed vulnerabilities by CWE and OWASP Top 10, evaluated LLM-assisted remediation, and proposed a secure AI-driven SDLC mapped to NIST SP 800-218 and the NIST AI RMF. Sole author.

UNDER REVIEW · 2026 · REVIEWED BY DR. ALMAS AMJAD AND DR. SAJID FAROOQ
